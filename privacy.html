Privacy Policy

Board Game Soundbox · Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Board Game Soundbox ("the App") is a soundboard application for tabletop role-playing games, developed by Sebastián Andaur ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

2. Information We Collect

Account information. If you choose to use the Community features (sharing sound bundles, rating, commenting), the App uses Firebase Authentication. Depending on your sign-in method, we may receive your display name and email address. You are not required to create an account to use the core soundboard features.

Community content. When you publish a sound bundle to the Community, the bundle data (name, description, tags, sound configuration) and your display name are stored in Google Firebase Firestore so other users can discover and download it.

Locally stored data. Sound files, bundles, mixes, and your preferences are stored on your device. We do not have access to this data unless you explicitly share it through the Community feature.

3. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage statistics.
  • We do not collect crash reports.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or any sensor data.
  • We do not use advertising SDKs or serve ads.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: to identify your account in the Community tab.
  • Community features: to display your published bundles, comments, and ratings to other users.

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google) — for authentication and cloud data storage.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your local data remains on your device until you uninstall the App or clear its data. Community content you publish is stored in Firebase until you delete it from within the App. You can delete your account and all associated data directly from the app. See our Account Deletion page for full details on what is deleted and what is kept.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard security measures provided by Google Firebase to protect your data in transit and at rest. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
